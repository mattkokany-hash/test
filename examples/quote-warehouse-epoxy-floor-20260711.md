## Scope of work

We will supply and apply a hard-wearing, anti-slip epoxy floor coating system to
your **300 m² warehouse floor**. Work begins with full mechanical **diamond
grinding** to remove existing contamination, laitance and coatings, opening the
concrete profile for maximum adhesion. Any minor cracks, spalls and joints are
patched and made good.

We then apply a **penetrating epoxy primer/sealer** followed by **two high-build
epoxy coats**, with an **anti-slip aggregate** broadcast into the surface for
safe footing in wet or oily conditions. The finished floor is seamless, chemical-
and abrasion-resistant, easy to clean, and built for forklift and pallet traffic.

All grinding waste and wash-water are contained and removed, and the site is left
clean and ready for return to service after the recommended cure period.
