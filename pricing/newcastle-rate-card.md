# Newcastle Rate Card — reference & cost drivers

> **This is a checklist of what to price and where the numbers come from — NOT a
> price list.** The `newcastle-pricing-superagent` must fetch the *current*
> average Newcastle figure for each driver at request time. Any indicative
> ranges below are only to sanity-check live search results, not to quote from.
> Region defaults to **Newcastle, NSW, Australia (AUD, GST 10%)**.

## Cost drivers to research for every job

### Labour
- Tradesperson / painter / applicator hourly rate (Newcastle NSW)
- Labourer / offsider hourly rate
- Crew size and productivity (m² per hour for prep vs application)
- Travel / mobilisation time

### Materials & consumables (get current per-litre / per-pack price)
- **Coatings:** primers (zinc/epoxy), intermediate coats, top coats
  (polyurethane, epoxy floor e.g. Dulux Luxafloor, Jotun, industrial enamels)
- Coverage / spread rate (m² per litre) per product — drives quantity
- Wastage allowance (typically 10–15%)
- Thinners / hardeners / additives (anti-slip, UV, colour packs)
- Abrasive media (garnet/soda) for blasting
- Masking, tape, plastic, filler, sealant, cleaning chemicals

### Equipment & plant (day-hire rates)
- Pressure washer / hot-water unit
- Airless spray rig / compressor
- Blasting pot & recovery
- Generator

### Access
- Scaffold hire
- EWP / boom / scissor lift hire (day rate + delivery)
- Traffic management (if roadside)

### Waste & disposal
- Skip / bin hire
- Spent abrasive / hazardous / wash-water disposal
- Containment (bunding) materials

### Travel & call-out
- Distance from base (km), fuel, minimum call-out fee

### Overheads, margin, contingency (applied as %, stated on internal sheet)
- Overhead recovery %
- Profit margin % (typical trade range 15–30% on cost)
- Contingency % (5–10%, higher for exterior/weather-exposed work)

## Search queries that work well
- `pressure washing cost per m2 Newcastle NSW <year>`
- `epoxy floor coating price per m2 Newcastle`
- `sandblasting cost per m2 Newcastle`
- `painter hourly rate Newcastle NSW <year>`
- `<product name> price 15L Bunbury/Newcastle` (product prices are fairly national)
- `skip bin hire Newcastle price`
- `EWP / boom lift hire Newcastle per day`

## Rules
1. Always cite source + date seen for each adopted figure.
2. If sources disagree, use the median and note the range.
3. If no Newcastle figure exists, use nearest regional/national and flag
   `[assumption]`.
4. Never quote from this file's ranges alone — they are a reality check only.
